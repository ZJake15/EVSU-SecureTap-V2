2. WHAT SECURETAP IS - AND WHAT IT IS NOT

SecureTap is a capstone project that helps campus security staff see who
enters and leaves a gate, using face recognition, school ID cards and
manual entries by guards.

SecureTap IS:
  - a tool that HELPS guards and the Office of Student Affairs and Services
    (SASO) at the gate
  - a record of entries and exits for campus security
  - a program that keeps all its Data on the computer it is installed on

SecureTap is NOT:
  - a replacement for security guards or their judgment
  - a door, turnstile or lock controller - it does not open or close
    anything by itself
  - an emergency, fire or life-safety system
  - a system for attendance grades, discipline decisions or employment
    decisions on its own
  - a video recording (CCTV) system
  - a finished commercial product; it is provided for study,
    demonstration and evaluation (see section 4)

6. ACCOUNTS, ROLES AND PASSWORDS

6.1 Roles
  Admin              manages accounts and settings, approves or rejects
                     deactivations, can permanently delete persons, and
                     sees everything, including the whole Audit Log
  SASO               registers and manages students and staff, sees
                     records from all gates and reports, and asks for
                     deactivations
  Security Officer   works at one assigned gate, sees that gate's records
                     for today, and records manual entries

The University decides who gets which role. Give each User the LOWEST
role they need, and only the gates they work at.

6.2 Passwords and accounts
  - Every User has their OWN account. Accounts must never be shared,
    lent or used by anyone else.
  - Passwords must be at least 10 characters. Use a password that is not
    used anywhere else and is hard to guess.
  - Never write your password where others can see it, tell it to anyone,
    or let the browser save it on a shared computer.
  - Sign out (or lock the computer) when you leave it.
  - If you think someone knows your password, change it at once and tell
    the Admin.
  - You are responsible for everything done with your account, unless you
    reported it as lost or misused before it happened.

6.3 What the Admin must do
  - Create accounts only for authorized staff, with the right role and
    gate.
  - Deactivate an account the same day its User leaves the job, changes
    role, or should no longer have access.
  - Review accounts regularly (at least every term) and remove ones that
    are not needed.
  - Consider turning on, on the Settings page, the failed-login lockout
    and the automatic logout.
  - Keep at least two people able to act as Admin, so the University is
    never locked out, but no more than needed.


7. STAFF ID CARDS AND SIGNING IN AT THE GATE

  - When the Admin turns on gate sign-in, the guard on duty signs in at
    the gate monitor with their password or by tapping their own staff ID
    card, so every entry shows who was on duty.
  - A staff ID card is as personal as a password: never lend it, never
    tap it for someone else, and report a lost card to the Admin at once
    so it can be removed from the account.
  - Sign out at the end of your shift. The System signs a guard out by
    itself after the shift length set by the Admin.
  - Entries made while nobody is signed in are marked Unattended. A gate
    should not be left unattended unless the University allows it.


8. BEFORE THE SYSTEM IS USED AT A GATE

Before SecureTap is used with real students and staff, the University
must:
  - have its Data Protection Officer (DPO) review the System, these Terms
    and the Privacy Policy, and carry out a Privacy Impact Assessment
  - approve a written consent form for registering faces
  - put up a clear notice at each gate, where people see it BEFORE they
    reach the camera, saying that face recognition is in use, why, how
    long information is kept and whom to contact
  - decide how people who do not consent, or who cannot be recognized,
    are let in (for example, by a guard checking their ID by hand)
  - choose retention periods and turn on "Automatic deletion" on the
    Settings page (see the Privacy Policy, section 11)
  - register the System with the National Privacy Commission if required
  - train the Users who will run it


9. REGISTERING STUDENTS AND STAFF

  - Register a person's face ONLY after they (and, for a minor, a parent
    or guardian) have given written consent. Keep the signed form as the
    University requires.
  - Take registration photos only with the person present and aware, in
    a respectful way, following the on-screen guides. Never use photos
    taken from social media, ID pictures taken for another purpose, or
    photos taken without the person knowing.
  - Type names, ID numbers and card numbers carefully, and fix mistakes as
    soon as you find them.
  - A distinguishing note (for look-alikes) must be short, respectful and
    only about telling two people apart - never about health, religion,
    ethnicity, disability or anything insulting.
  - When importing many people at once (bulk import), make sure every
    person in the file has given consent, and delete the import file and
    its photos from the computer afterwards.
  - Deactivate people who should no longer enter, and permanently delete
    those who have left, as the University's rules say.


10. USING THE GATE MONITOR

  - The gate monitor helps the guard; the guard stays in charge. Watch
    the screen and the people at the gate.
  - When the System says "not recognized", "suspected fake" or asks for a
    card, check the person's ID before letting them in or turning them
    away.
  - Record a manual entry only after you have checked the person's
    identity yourself, and always give a true reason.
  - Treat everyone with respect. A gate alert is not proof of wrongdoing.
  - Do not point the camera anywhere other than the gate lane - not at
    offices, classrooms, restrooms, homes or other private places.
  - Keep the screen where people passing by cannot read names or see
    photos of others.
  - Do not cover, move or change the camera or card reader in a way the
    University has not approved.


11. USING THE DASHBOARD

  - Open the dashboard only on the computer where SecureTap is installed,
    and only for your work.
  - Look up a person or record only when your job needs it - never out of
    curiosity, or to follow, watch or check on someone for personal
    reasons.
  - Change settings only if you understand what they do. Settings marked
    as risky (for example, turning off the spoof check) weaken security;
    change them only with the University's approval.
  - Approve or reject deactivation requests fairly, and give a reason.
  - Do not delete records to hide a mistake, an incident or misuse.

21. MISTAKES BY THE SYSTEM

Face recognition, spoof checks and card readers are not perfect. SecureTap
can:
  - fail to recognize a registered person ("not recognized")
  - rarely, mistake one person for another, especially look-alikes or
    twins
  - mistake a real face for a photo or screen, or the other way round
  - miss or misread a card tap
  - record a wrong time if the computer's clock is wrong

To reduce harm, the System asks for a card tap when it is unsure, guards
check IDs before acting on an alert, and no decision about a person
should be based on the System alone. Keep the computer's date and time
correct.
