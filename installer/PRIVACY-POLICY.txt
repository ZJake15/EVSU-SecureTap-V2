1. WORDS USED IN THIS POLICY

  "System" or "SecureTap"  EVSU SecureTap: the gate monitor, the dashboard,
                           the setup window and the program behind them,
                           installed on this computer.
  "University" or "EVSU"   Eastern Visayas State University, the campus
                           that operates the System.
  "Person"                 A student or employee registered in the System
                           so they can be recognized at the gate.
  "Visitor"                Anyone seen at the gate who is NOT registered.
  "User"                   A staff member who signs in to the dashboard or
                           the gate monitor: an Admin, a SASO account
                           (Security Manager) or a Security Officer
                           (guard).
  "Face fingerprint"       A list of 512 numbers calculated from a face
                           photo. It describes the face well enough to
                           compare it with another photo. The photo cannot
                           be rebuilt from it. (Also called a "face
                           embedding" or "face template".)
  "Entry record"           One line in the records: who passed the gate,
                           when, at which gate, in which direction and how
                           they were checked.
  "Personal information"   Any information that identifies a person, or
                           can be used to identify them, as defined in the
                           Data Privacy Act.


2. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE INFORMATION

The University is the PERSONAL INFORMATION CONTROLLER: it decides why and
how the System is used, who is registered, and how long records are kept.
Through its Data Protection Officer (DPO), the University answers questions
and requests about the information in the System.

The people who installed SecureTap and the staff who use it act on the
University's behalf and must follow this policy and the University's own
data privacy rules.

The SecureTap capstone team wrote the program. The team does NOT receive,
see or keep any of the information collected by an installed copy (see
section 10).


3. WHAT THIS SYSTEM DOES

EVSU SecureTap checks who enters and leaves a campus gate:

  - A camera at the gate looks at the faces of people passing. Each face is
    turned into a face fingerprint and compared with the face fingerprints
    of registered students and staff.
  - The System also checks that the face is a real person and not a
    printed photo or a phone or tablet screen ("spoof check"), and asks
    people to uncover their face if a hand, mask or scarf covers it.
  - A school ID card can be tapped on a card reader when the System asks
    for it - for example, when it is unsure, or for look-alikes and twins.
  - A guard can record an entry by hand when the camera or card reader
    cannot be used.
  - Each entry or exit is recorded, and the guard on duty sees the result
    on the gate monitor.

The camera checks EVERY face it sees at the gate - not only people who tap
a card. This means visitors and passers-by are also seen by the camera. A
clear notice must be posted at the gate, where people can read it BEFORE
they reach the camera, saying that face recognition is in use, why, and
whom to contact (see section 18).


4. WHAT INFORMATION IT COLLECTS

4.1 About registered students and staff (Persons)
  - Full name
  - Student or employee ID number
  - Role (student, faculty, staff, and so on)
  - Department or course
  - School ID card number (the number stored on the card's chip)
  - Face photos taken during registration: usually 5 photos (front,
    slightly left, slightly right, no expression, smiling), or 1 photo if
    the single-photo option is used, or photos brought in by a bulk import
  - One face fingerprint for each registration photo, and a note of
    whether that photo was of low quality
  - Whether the person is active (allowed in) or deactivated
  - A short "distinguishing note", only if staff add one - for example, to
    help a guard tell two look-alikes apart
  - Look-alike pairs: when two registered people's faces are very similar,
    the System records the pair and how similar they are, so it can ask
    them for a card tap
  - Deactivation requests about the person: who asked, when, why, and the
    Admin's decision

4.2 At the gate (for everyone the camera sees)
  - Each entry and exit: who (if recognized), date and time, which gate,
    the direction (entry or exit), how they were checked (face, card,
    face and card, or a guard's manual entry), the result (allowed,
    refused, not recognized, suspected fake, and so on) and the reason
  - Technical details of the check: how closely the face matched, the
    real-face (spoof check) score, and whether the face looked covered
  - Which guard was on duty for that entry, or that the gate was
    unattended, and which guard recorded a manual entry
  - A PHOTO of a face that was NOT recognized, or that looked like a
    printed photo or a phone screen, so a guard or SASO can see who was
    turned away
  - For a face that was not recognized, its face fingerprint, kept for a
    short time to tell whether the same unknown person keeps returning to
    the gate. It is always deleted after 1 day.
  - Short-lived working data while a person is being checked (match
    attempts, spoof-check attempts, covered-face attempts, a pending card
    request). These are deleted after 1 day.
  - If the gate monitor briefly cannot reach the program behind it, card
    taps waiting to be recorded (card number, gate, direction and time)
    are kept in a small queue on this computer and recorded as soon as it
    can. Faces are never queued.

Faces of RECOGNIZED people are NOT saved as photos at the gate. Camera
frames are only looked at in the computer's memory and thrown away.

4.3 About staff who use the System (Users)
  - Username, name, role and assigned gate
  - Password - stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; nobody, not even the
    Admin, can read it
  - Staff ID card number, if one is added for signing in at the gate
  - How many wrong passwords were entered in a row, and until when the
    account is locked (only if the lockout setting is on)
  - Gate shifts: who signed in at which gate, when, how (password or card),
    and when and why the shift ended
  - The Audit Log: a record of important actions, with who did it and
    when - creating, changing or deactivating accounts, account lockouts,
    deactivation requests and the Admin's decisions, permanently deleting
    a person, marking or unmarking look-alike pairs, settings changes (with
    the old and new value), automatic deletions, manual entries, and gate
    sign-ins and sign-outs. Ordinary viewing is not logged.

4.4 About this computer
  - The camera and card reader chosen, the gate name and direction, and a
    speed test result for this computer. These are about the computer, not
    about people.


5. WHAT IT DOES NOT COLLECT

SecureTap does NOT collect or keep:
  - video recordings - the camera feed is never recorded
  - photos of recognized people at the gate
  - sound - the microphone is never used
  - location (GPS), contacts, or anything from people's phones
  - grades, health information, religion, home address or phone numbers
  - anything typed or done on the computer outside SecureTap
  - any information for advertising or tracking across websites


6. WHY IT IS COLLECTED, AND THE LEGAL BASIS

The information is used ONLY for campus security:
  - to know who entered and left the campus, and when
  - to stop people from using someone else's ID card, photo or face
  - to help guards and the Office of Student Affairs and Services (SASO)
    respond to incidents and check reports
  - to keep the System accurate (for example, the accuracy report on the
    Reports page, which is calculated from the stored face fingerprints)
  - to keep a record of what staff did in the System, so mistakes and
    misuse can be found

It is NOT used for grades, advertising, profiling, or any purpose other
than campus security, and it is NOT sold, rented or traded.

Face photos and face fingerprints are biometric information. SecureTap
treats them with the same care the Data Privacy Act requires for sensitive
personal information. The University relies on the written consent of
each registered person (section 13) and on its responsibility to keep the
campus safe. The University's DPO confirms the exact legal basis and
records it in the University's own privacy notices.


7. HOW FACE RECOGNITION WORKS, STEP BY STEP

  1. Registration: staff take the person's photos with the dashboard. The
     System checks each photo's quality, makes a face fingerprint from it,
     and warns staff if the person looks very similar to someone already
     registered.
  2. At the gate: the camera finds faces in the picture. Blurry frames,
     faces turned too far, faces cut off at the edge and covered faces are
     skipped.
  3. The System checks that the face is a real person, not a photo or a
     screen.
  4. It makes a face fingerprint of the face and compares it with the
     registered ones. Several frames in a row must agree before it
     decides.
  5. If it is sure, it records the entry. If it is unsure, or the person
     has a look-alike, it asks for a card tap. If the face is not
     recognized, the entry is recorded as not recognized, with a photo for
     the guard.
  6. Everything happens on THIS computer. No face, photo or fingerprint is
     sent to any online service, and no outside company's face
     recognition service is used.

Face recognition is never perfect. The System can occasionally fail to
recognize a registered person, or - rarely - mistake one person for
another. Section 20 explains what is done about that.


8. WHERE THE INFORMATION IS KEPT

Everything stays on THIS COMPUTER only, in the folder
%LOCALAPPDATA%\EVSU SecureTap (the program itself is in a separate folder
and holds no personal information). Nothing is sent to the internet, to a
cloud service, or to the SecureTap capstone team.

The gate monitor and the dashboard talk to the program behind them inside
this computer only; they are not reachable from other computers on the
network.

The only internet use: when the computer is online, the dashboard
downloads its text fonts from Google Fonts. No personal information is sent
with that request, but, like any website visit, Google can see that this
computer asked for the fonts.


9. WHO CAN SEE IT

Only signed-in Users, each limited by their role:

  Admin
    - everything: persons, photos, records from all gates, reports,
      settings, accounts and the whole Audit Log
    - the only role that can permanently delete a person, approve or
      reject a deactivation, manage accounts and change settings

  SASO (Security Manager)
    - registers, edits and views students and staff, their photos and
      look-alike pairs
    - views entry records from all gates, and reports
    - asks to deactivate a person (an Admin has to approve it)
    - sees only their OWN actions in the Audit Log

  Security Officer (guard)
    - sees records for their own assigned gate only, and only today's
    - records manual entries at the gate
    - cannot see the list of registered persons, reports, settings or
      accounts

At the gate monitor, the guard on duty sees the name and result of each
person checked, and the photo of an unrecognized face or suspected fake.

Photos can only be opened through the dashboard by a signed-in User, using
a link that stops working after a short time.

The people who look after this computer (for example, whoever knows its
Windows password) could reach the data folder directly. That is why
section 18 asks for the computer to be protected.


10. SHARING WITH OTHERS

The System itself never shares information with anyone. The University
may share information from it only:
  - with the person it is about, when they ask (section 15)
  - inside the University, with offices that need it for campus security
    or discipline under the University's own rules
  - with the police, a court or another government body, when the law
    requires it or a lawful order asks for it
  - in an emergency that threatens someone's life or safety

Any sharing must be decided by the University (through its DPO or another
authorized office) - not by an individual User - and should be recorded.

The SecureTap capstone team does not receive any information from an
installed copy and cannot see it.


11. HOW LONG IT IS KEPT

The Admin decides how long records are kept, on the dashboard's Settings
page (Privacy & Data Retention). "Automatic deletion" is OFF until the
Admin turns it on. Once it is on, the System deletes, every day:

  What                                 Default        Can be set to
  -----------------------------------  -------------  ------------------
  Entry and exit records               365 days       7 to 3650 days
  Gate photos (unknown faces and       90 days        1 to 3650 days
  suspected fakes) - the record stays
  Audit Log entries                    kept forever   0 (forever) to
                                                      36500 days

ALWAYS, even with "Automatic deletion" off:
  - face fingerprints of people who were not recognized are deleted after
    1 day (these people never agreed to have their face stored)
  - the short-lived working data from checking faces (section 4.2) is
    deleted after 1 day
  - photo files that no longer belong to any record are deleted

A registered person's photos and face fingerprints are kept for as long as
they are registered (see section 12). Replacing a photo deletes the old
one.

The University should choose retention periods no longer than it really
needs, write them in its records retention schedule, and turn on
"Automatic deletion" so they are followed. Until it is turned on, records
are kept until someone deletes them.


12. WHAT HAPPENS WHEN A PERSON LEAVES OR IS REMOVED

  Deactivating a person
    They are no longer allowed in at the gate. Their record, photos and
    face fingerprints stay, so they can be reactivated (for example, after
    a leave of absence). A SASO deactivation needs an Admin's approval.

  Permanently deleting a person (Admin only)
    Deletes their record, their face fingerprints, all their registration
    photos and their look-alike pairs. This cannot be undone. Their past
    entry records stay, but without their name ("Unknown"), so gate
    statistics remain correct. Those past records are then deleted
    according to section 11.

When a student graduates, transfers or leaves, or an employee leaves, the
University should permanently delete them within a reasonable time,
unless it has a lawful reason to keep their information longer.


13. CONSENT

Students and staff must be told how the System works (for example, by
giving them this policy) and must give their WRITTEN consent before their
face is registered. The consent form is the University's, approved by its
DPO.

  - Consent is voluntary. A person who does not consent is not registered
    and must not be refused entry only for that reason. The University
    decides how they are let in instead - for example, by a guard checking
    their ID by hand and recording a manual entry.
  - A person can WITHDRAW consent at any time by telling the SASO or the
    DPO. Their face photos and face fingerprints are then deleted (the
    Admin permanently deletes their registration), and they use another
    way in.
  - Withdrawing consent does not make earlier, lawful use unlawful.

Visitors cannot practically give consent before the camera sees them.
That is why the System keeps as little as possible about them: their face
fingerprint is deleted after 1 day, and only a photo of the attempt is
kept for the gate photo period. The gate notice (section 18) tells them
this before they reach the camera.


14. CHILDREN AND MINORS

Some students may be under 18. For them, the consent of a parent or legal
guardian should be obtained in addition to the student's own agreement,
following the University's rules. Their information gets at least the same
protection as everyone else's.


15. YOUR RIGHTS AS A DATA SUBJECT

Under the Data Privacy Act, every person whose information is in the
System - students, staff, Users and visitors - has the right to:

  - BE INFORMED that their information is being collected and how it is
    used (this policy and the gate notice)
  - ACCESS the information kept about them - for example, their record,
    their registration photos and their entry records - and get a copy
  - CORRECT wrong or outdated information, such as a misspelled name or a
    wrong ID number
  - have their information ERASED or BLOCKED when it is no longer needed,
    was collected unlawfully, or consent is withdrawn
  - OBJECT to the use of their information, including face recognition
  - DATA PORTABILITY: get their information in a common electronic format
    (the dashboard can export entry records as a CSV file)
  - be paid DAMAGES for harm caused by wrong, false or unlawfully obtained
    or used personal information
  - FILE A COMPLAINT with the National Privacy Commission

These rights may be limited only where the law allows, for example when
the information is needed for a pending investigation.


16. HOW TO MAKE A REQUEST OR A COMPLAINT

  1. Contact the EVSU Data Protection Officer through the University's
     official channels, or the Office of Student Affairs and Services
     (SASO). Say who you are and what you are asking for.
  2. The University may ask you to prove who you are, so that nobody else
     can get your information.
  3. The University should answer within a reasonable time, and within
     the period the law or NPC rules set.
  4. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the National Privacy
     Commission (www.privacy.gov.ph).

Users of the System must not answer requests on their own. Pass them to
the DPO or SASO.


17. HOW THE INFORMATION IS PROTECTED

  - Everything is processed and stored on this computer only; nothing is
    sent online (section 8)
  - Passwords are stored scrambled (bcrypt) and must be at least 10
    characters
  - Each role sees only what it needs (section 9); deactivating someone as
    a SASO needs an Admin's approval
  - Important actions are recorded in the Audit Log
  - Optional on the Settings page: lock an account after repeated wrong
    passwords, and log Users out automatically when inactive
  - Optional: guards sign in at the gate monitor, so every entry shows who
    was on duty; entries with nobody signed in are marked Unattended
  - Photos can only be opened with a short-lived signed link
  - Error pages never show personal information or the System's internal
    details
  - Unknown faces' fingerprints and other working data are deleted after
    1 day, and leftover photo files are cleaned up automatically
  - The program and the data are kept in separate folders; installing an
    update never touches the data

No system is perfectly secure. Section 18 lists what Users and the
University must do so these protections work.


18. YOUR OWN DUTIES AS A USER OF THIS SYSTEM

Everyone who installs or uses SecureTap must:
  - protect this computer: use a Windows password, do not share the
    Windows account, lock the screen when away, keep Windows updated, and
    turn on BitLocker drive encryption if available
  - keep their own SecureTap password secret and never share their
    account or staff ID card
  - look at personal information ONLY when their work needs it
  - never copy, photograph, screenshot, print, post or send photos,
    names or records from the System, except as the University's rules
    allow
  - post the gate notice where people see it before reaching the camera
  - collect written consent before registering anyone's face
  - tell the Admin and the DPO at once if they think information was lost,
    stolen, seen by the wrong person or misused (section 19)

The Terms of Service explain these duties in more detail.


19. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG (A DATA BREACH)

A data breach is when personal information is lost, stolen, destroyed,
changed, or seen by someone who should not see it - for example, if this
computer is stolen, or someone copies the data folder.

If this happens:
  1. Tell the Admin and the University's DPO IMMEDIATELY.
  2. Do not delete anything or try to hide it; write down what happened
     (what, when, who noticed).
  3. If you can do so safely, stop it from getting worse - for example,
     change passwords, or disconnect the computer from the network.
  4. The DPO decides what else to do, including telling the National
     Privacy Commission and the people affected within the time the NPC
     rules require (72 hours from knowing about a breach that must be
     reported).


20. ACCURACY AND MISTAKES

  - Staff must type names and ID numbers carefully and correct mistakes
    as soon as they are found.
  - Face recognition can make mistakes. A gate decision is NOT final: a
    guard can always check a person's ID and let them in by hand (which is
    recorded), and no one should be punished only because of a gate
    record without a human checking it first.
  - People who are often not recognized can have their photos taken
    again.
  - The System asks for a card tap when it is unsure, and by default
    always asks look-alikes and twins for a card.


21. EXPORTED FILES, BACKUPS AND COPIES

When a User exports records (for example, a CSV file of entry records) or
prints anything from the System, that copy is no longer protected or
automatically deleted by SecureTap. The User and the University are
responsible for it: keep it only as long as needed, store it safely, and
delete it when done.

When data is brought over from an older copy of SecureTap, the older copy
is left unchanged, and any data this copy had before is kept as a backup
file (db.sqlite3.bak) in the data folder. Delete the older copy and the
backup once they are no longer needed - Automatic deletion does not clean
them.


22. MOVING THE DATA TO ANOTHER COMPUTER

The data folder can only be moved to another computer with the
University's approval, on a protected (encrypted) drive, and must be
deleted from any drive used to carry it. The old computer's copy should be
deleted once the move is confirmed.


23. UNINSTALLING

Uninstalling (Settings > Apps > EVSU SecureTap > Uninstall) asks whether to
delete the data too. Choose Yes when the computer is given away, sold,
repaired by outsiders or retired, so no personal information is left
behind. Choose No only to install SecureTap again on the same computer.

Note: deleting files does not always erase them from the drive
completely. Before giving away or retiring a computer that held SecureTap
data, the drive should be securely wiped (or Windows reset with the
"clean the drive" option).


24. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This policy may be updated when SecureTap changes or the law changes. A
new version comes with each new installer and replaces this one; the
version number is at the top. Important changes should be announced to
Users and registered persons.


25. IMPORTANT NOTE ABOUT THIS PROJECT

EVSU SecureTap is a capstone project. Before it is used at a real campus
gate, the University's Data Protection Officer should:
  - review this policy and the System
  - carry out a Privacy Impact Assessment
  - approve the consent form, the gate notice and the retention periods
  - register the System with the NPC if required

A copy of this policy is installed with the app (Start menu: "EVSU
SecureTap Privacy Policy").
