EVSU SECURETAP - PRIVACY POLICY
Campus entry monitoring system, Eastern Visayas State University

Please read this before installing. By installing and using EVSU SecureTap,
you agree to handle the personal information it collects as described here,
in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).


1. WHAT THIS SYSTEM DOES

EVSU SecureTap checks who enters and leaves a campus gate. A camera at the
gate looks at the faces of people passing and compares them with the photos
of registered students and staff. A school ID card can also be tapped on a
card reader. Each entry or exit is recorded.

The camera checks EVERY face it sees at the gate - not only people who tap a
card. A clear notice must be posted at the gate so everyone knows that face
recognition is in use.


2. WHAT INFORMATION IT COLLECTS

About registered students and staff:
  - Name, student or employee ID number, role, and department or course
  - School ID card number
  - Face photos taken during registration
  - A "face fingerprint": a list of numbers calculated from each photo, used
    to recognize the face. The photo cannot be rebuilt from it.

At the gate:
  - Each entry and exit: who, when, which gate, and how they were checked
    (face, card, or a guard's manual entry)
  - A photo of a face that was NOT recognized, or that looked like a printed
    photo or a phone screen, so a guard can see who was turned away
  - For a face that was not recognized, its face fingerprint for a short
    time, to tell whether the same person is still at the gate. It is
    always deleted after 1 day.

About staff who use the dashboard (Admin, SASO, Security Officers):
  - Username, name, role and assigned gate
  - Password - stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; nobody can read it
  - Staff ID card number, if one is added for signing in at the gate
  - A record of important actions (the Audit Log), such as account changes,
    setting changes, manual entries and gate sign-ins


3. WHY IT IS COLLECTED

Only to keep the campus secure: to know who entered and left, to stop people
using someone else's ID or photo, and to help guards and the Office of
Student Affairs respond to incidents. The information is not used for
grades, advertising, or any other purpose, and it is not sold or shared.


4. WHERE IT IS KEPT

Everything stays on THIS COMPUTER only, in the folder
%LOCALAPPDATA%\EVSU SecureTap. Nothing is sent to the internet or to any
online service. (When the computer is online, the dashboard downloads its
text fonts from Google Fonts; no personal information is sent with that.)

Keep this computer protected: use a Windows password, do not share the
Windows account, and turn on BitLocker drive encryption if available.


5. WHO CAN SEE IT

Only signed-in dashboard users, each limited by their role:
  - Admin: everything, including accounts, settings and the Audit Log
  - SASO (Security Manager): students and staff, records and reports
  - Security Officer (guard): today's records for their own gate only
Photos can only be opened through the dashboard by a signed-in user.


6. HOW LONG IT IS KEPT

The Admin decides how long entry records, gate photos and the Audit Log are
kept, on the dashboard's Settings page (Privacy & Data Retention). Once
"Automatic deletion" is switched on, older records and gate photos are
deleted every day. It is off until the Admin switches it on.

Always, even with "Automatic deletion" off:
  - face fingerprints of people who were not recognized are deleted after
    1 day (these people never agreed to have their face stored)
  - photo files that no longer belong to any record are deleted

A registered person's photos and face data are kept while they are
registered. Permanently deleting a person deletes their record, their face
data and all their photos. Their past entry records stay, without their
name. Replacing a photo deletes the old one.


7. YOUR RIGHTS

Under the Data Privacy Act, students and staff have the right to:
  - be told how their information is used (this policy)
  - see the information kept about them
  - have wrong information corrected
  - ask for their information to be removed, or object to its use
  - file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission
To use these rights, contact the EVSU Data Protection Officer through the
university's official channels.


8. CONSENT

Students and staff must give their written consent before their face is
registered. The university decides how people who do not consent are let
in - for example, by a guard checking their ID by hand.


9. KEEPING IT SAFE

  - Passwords are stored scrambled (bcrypt) and must be at least 10
    characters
  - Each role sees only what it needs; important actions are recorded
  - Optional on the Settings page: lock an account after repeated wrong
    passwords, and log users out automatically when inactive
  - Photos can only be opened with a short-lived signed link
  - Error pages never show personal information or the system's
    internal details


10. IMPORTANT NOTE

EVSU SecureTap is a capstone project. Before it is used at a real campus
gate, the university's Data Protection Officer should review this policy and
the system, and approve the consent form, the gate notice and the retention
periods.

A copy of this policy is installed with the app (Start menu: "EVSU SecureTap
Privacy Policy").
