# How to Install EVSU SecureTap

You only need **one file**: `EVSU-SecureTap-Setup.exe`. You don't need the internet or any other program.

---

## Step 1 — Get the installer

Get `EVSU-SecureTap-Setup.exe` from your team (for example, on a USB stick) and copy it to the computer.

## Step 2 — Run it

1. **Double-click** `EVSU-SecureTap-Setup.exe`.
2. If a blue box says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This is normal.
3. Read the **Privacy Policy** (how the app handles face photos and records), choose **I have read and accept the privacy policy**, and click **Next**.
4. Click **Next** and then **Install**.
5. Leave **"Open EVSU SecureTap now"** checked and click **Finish**.

You can read the privacy policy again any time: Start menu → **EVSU SecureTap Privacy Policy**.

## Step 3 — First-time setup

A setup window opens. Follow it:

1. **Welcome** — click **Start**.
2. **Your data:**
   - This computer has an **older SecureTap** with students already registered → choose **Bring the data from an older copy**. Nobody has to register again.
   - This is a **new** computer → choose **Start empty**.
3. **Admin account** — type a username and password. Write them down; you'll use them to log in.
   (If you brought old data over, this step is skipped — use your old login.)
4. **Camera and card reader** — plug them in. If one is missing, you can still continue.
5. **Speed test** — click **Run the speed test**, wait about 30 seconds, then click **Finish**.

## Step 4 — Use it

Open **EVSU SecureTap** from the icon on your desktop. Then choose:

- **Dashboard** — to register students, see records and reports.
- **Entry Agent** — to open the gate camera and card reader.

That's it!

---

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Windows protected your PC" | Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. |
| Bringing the old data over fails | Close the old SecureTap, restart the computer, then open EVSU SecureTap and try again. Your old data is never changed. |
| Camera not found | Plug it in. It's picked up on its own. |
| Card reader not found | Plug it in, then open Entry Agent again. |
| It feels slow | Plug the laptop in and close other apps. |

## To remove it

**Settings → Apps → EVSU SecureTap → Uninstall.** It asks whether to delete the data too. Choose **No** if you might install it again; everything comes back when you do.
